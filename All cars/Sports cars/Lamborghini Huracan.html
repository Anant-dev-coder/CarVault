Lamborghini Huracan

Engine & Performance

  • Engine: 5.2-litre (5,204 cc) naturally aspirated V10

  • Power: 610–640 bhp (depending on variant)

  • Torque: 560–600 Nm

  • 0–100 km/h: ~2.9–3.4 s (variant-dependent)

  • Top speed: 325 km/h

Transmission & Drivetrain

  • Transmission: 7-speed dual-clutch automatic

  • Drivetrain: All-wheel drive (AWD) or Rear-wheel drive (RWD) depending on variant

Dimensions & Weight

  • Length × Width × Height: 4,459 × 1,924 × 1,165 mm

  • Wheelbase: 2,620 mm

  • Kerb weight: ~1,422–1,542 kg (variant-dependent)

  • Boot: 110 litres (front) + 100 litres (rear)

  • Fuel tank: 83 litres
