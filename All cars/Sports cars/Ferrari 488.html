Ferrari 488

Engine & Performance

  • Engine: 3.9-litre (3,902 cc) twin-turbocharged V8

  • Power: 661 bhp

  • Torque: 730 Nm

  • 0–100 km/h: ~3.0 s

  • Top speed: 330 km/h

Transmission & Drivetrain

  • Transmission: 7-speed dual-clutch automatic

  • Drivetrain: Rear-wheel drive (RWD)

Dimensions & Weight

  • Length × Width × Height: 4,519 × 1,852 × 1,300 mm

  • Wheelbase: 2,450 mm

  • Kerb weight: ~1,505–1,640 kg (depending on variant)

  • Boot: 132 litres (front) + 145 litres (rear)

  • Fuel tank: 64 litres
