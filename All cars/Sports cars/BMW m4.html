BMW M4

Engine & Performance

  • Engine: S58 3.0-litre (2993 cc) twin-turbocharged inline-6
  • M4 Competition: 503 bhp @ 6,250 rpm / 650 Nm @ 2,750 rpm
  • M4 CS: 543 bhp @ 6,250 rpm / 650 Nm @ 2,750 rpm
  • 0–100 km/h: ~3.8 s (Competition RWD) / ~3.4 s (xDrive)
  • Top speed: 250 km/h (electronically limited)

Transmission & Drivetrain

  • M4 Competition: 8-speed M Steptronic automatic, RWD or xDrive (AWD)

  • M4 CS: 8-speed automatic, xDrive (AWD)

  • Base M4 (US only): 6-speed manual, RWD

    Dimensions & Weight

  • Length × Width × Height: 4,794 × 1,887 × 1,397 mm

  • Wheelbase: 2,857 mm

  • Kerb weight: ~1,725–1,845 kg (variant-dependent)

  • Boot: 440 litres

  • Fuel tank: 59 litres
