Porsche 911

Engine & Performance

  • Engine: 3.0-litre (2,981 cc) twin-turbocharged flat-6

  • Power: 379–641 bhp (depending on variant)

  • Torque: 450–800 Nm (depending on variant)

  • 0–100 km/h: ~3.5–4.0 s (depending on variant)

  • Top speed: 293–330 km/h (depending on variant)

Transmission & Drivetrain

  • Transmission: 7-speed PDK (dual-clutch automatic)

  • Drivetrain: Rear-wheel drive (RWD) or All-wheel drive (AWD)

Dimensions & Weight

  • Length × Width × Height: 4,519 × 1,852 × 1,300 mm

  • Wheelbase: 2,450 mm

  • Kerb weight: ~1,505–1,640 kg (depending on variant)

  • Boot: 132 litres (front) + 145 litres (rear)

  • Fuel tank: 64 litres
